Image prompt used to make the bundled proxy reference (route B).
Regenerate a 2x2 four-view sheet with an image model, then split_views.py it,
then run check_facing.py on the result.

NOTE: the bundled mannequin in this folder is not made from this prompt any
more — it is drawn procedurally by ../../scripts/mannequin.py, because an image
model will not hold one skeleton across four views, nor keep the silhouette
identical while changing the material. Use this prompt when you need a proxy
whose weapon or proportions match a specific character, and treat the output as
a draft: check_facing.py decides whether it is usable.

WHY THIS PROMPT SPELLS OUT THE BACK
A reference whose front and back look the same makes the model turn the figure
around mid-clip: it reads the picture far more than the prompt, so "pure rear
view" in front of a frontal-looking drawing is a contradiction. The fix is a
*different material* on the back — never a different outline, because the
character pass inherits the proxy's silhouette.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Pixel art game sprite sheet, 2x2 grid of four panels on pure flat magenta
#FF00FF background, no white lines, no text, no logo, no watermark, no caption.
The same plain wooden artist's mannequin character in all four panels: pale
birch wood, round ball joints at shoulders elbows wrists hips knees ankles,
smooth featureless egg-shaped head with no face, no hair, no clothing, no armor.
It holds one straight grey steel short sword in each hand, blades angled down
and outward, the tips clear of the legs.
Keep identical body proportions, identical sword design and identical scale in
all four panels. Crisp hard-edged pixel clusters, limited flat palette, no
anti-aliasing, centered characters with margins.

THE FRONT AND THE BACK ARE DIFFERENT MATERIALS — this is the critical part:

TOP-LEFT  front view, facing the viewer, head-on, symmetric, both shoulders
          visible. Pale birch chest and face. A dark brown leather harness runs
          in a shallow V from each shoulder to a small brass ring at the centre
          of the sternum. A dark leather belt crosses the waist with a square
          brass buckle at its centre. Two toes visible on each foot.

TOP-RIGHT back view, we see the back of the head and the back of the torso,
          symmetric. A broad DARK BROWN LEATHER BACK PANEL covers the upper back
          from the shoulders to the waist, laced with a light cream X across the
          shoulder blades and a vertical spine seam down the centre. The lower
          back of the head is stained dark walnut, not pale birch. The belt is
          plain, with no buckle. Heels visible on both feet, no toes.

BOTTOM-LEFT  TRUE SIDE PROFILE facing image-LEFT: body rotated exactly 90
          degrees, only ONE arm and ONE leg visible, torso a thin vertical
          silhouette, head profile points left, the two swords overlap and point
          down-left. Seen from the side, the rear half of the torso is the dark
          leather panel and the front half is pale birch, and the back of the
          head is stained dark.

BOTTOM-RIGHT TRUE SIDE PROFILE facing image-RIGHT: the same, mirrored, head
          profile points right, the dark back panel and the stained nape are now
          on the right-hand side of each form.

The outline and the pose of the front and the back are IDENTICAL — the two sides
differ only in material and in the details listed above.
